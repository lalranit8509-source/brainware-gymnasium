A gym built for students, not for revenue

Brainware University Gymnasium is a campus fitness facility for every currently enrolled student — engineering, management, law, pharmacy and every other school on campus. There is no membership tier, no subscription and no equipment you have to pay to use. Show your ID, register once, and train under trainer supervision within your access window.

Student registration

Register once per semester. Your details go to the gym office and the trainer, and your name appears in the semester roll below.

Registration is free for all enrolled students. There is no membership or plan of any kind.

Student fitness requirements

What to bring, and what is expected of you once you are inside.

Before entering

1
University ID

Shown at the entry desk every visit.

2
Sports shoes

Closed, non-marking soles. Sandals are not allowed.

3
Gym clothes

Comfortable clothing you can move in freely.

4
Water bottle

Refill at the drinking water point inside.

5
Towel

Wipe down benches and machines after use.

Your responsibilities

✓
Maintain cleanliness

Leave every station the way you would want to find it.

✓
Return equipment

Racks and dumbbells go back to their marked place.

✓
Follow trainer instructions

Ask before attempting a new lift or machine.

✓
Warm up before exercise

Five to ten minutes, every session, without exception.

✓
Report damaged equipment

Tell the trainer immediately instead of using it.
